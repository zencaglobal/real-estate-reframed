Real Estate Reframed

Fourteen ways to separate the house, the investment, the financing, the lifestyle and the system around them — before putting them back together.

Price is not return. Asset return is not leveraged equity return. Wealth accumulation is not the same thing as investment performance. Housing consumption is not financial return. Non-financial utility is still real value. And the same physical asset can behave very differently inside different cities, financing systems, legal structures and household balance sheets.

It is not a case for property or against it. It is an attempt to become more precise about what job a property is doing — and what owning it will do to the rest of your financial life.
